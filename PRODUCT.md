# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences, weighted equally:
- Recruiters skimming between applications, checking in well under a minute that Kitty is real, relevant and worth a call.
- Hiring managers for marketing, organizational behaviour and HR roles who will open a case study and read the reasoning.

## Product Purpose
Kitty Gao's personal portfolio for recruiting. Success is that a visitor leaves **remembering her**: one clear, specific impression of who she is, not a generic "marketing student" site.

## Positioning
Psychology × business: Ivey HBA + Western Honours Specialization in Psychology (dual degree, 2028). She designs and markets with the "why" in mind — attention, memory and emotion — and has run real behavioural research. Few marketing candidates can show both her own design work and research on how people behave.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from her resume, LinkedIn or an application. Many will be on a laptop between other tasks; some on phones.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step; GitHub (ktbread/kitty-gao-portfolio) auto-deploys to Vercel.
- Existing features to keep available: poster case studies with notes, multi-page poster viewer, A/B test, bilingual (中文/EN) carousel, logo story, sketchbook drawer, walking-cat scroll progress, 404 lost cat.
- The "3-second test" section is to be replaced; replacement undecided.
- Open to: marketing roles (all, especially brand management and design-side), organizational behaviour and HR roles.

## Brand Commitments
- Logo is final: the "kitty gao" wordmark in Fraunces whose "o" carries joined cat ears and a curly Klein-blue tail; favicon is the cat-o without a face.
- Klein blue #002FA7 is the brand accent.
- Voice: warm, a little playful, plain-spoken ("Not Hello Kitty…", "the cat doesn't bite").

## Evidence on Hand
- Posters: Color Quest Autumn Picnic (EmpowerCHANGE Western, 2026), Puppy Therapy (解聆人 EmpowerCHANGE, 2023), Club Recruitment (EmpowerCHANGE Western, 2025–26, 40+ applicants), Now Hiring: Consultants (Viridian, 3 slides).
- Explainer carousel: Ontario's 4 mental health professionals, 7 slides in Chinese and English.
- Results: Viridian campaign 100K+ impressions, 45% follower growth, 150 applicants; MMHC 333% more shares, 256% more views; Ivey research 150+ participants, 96K+ data points; 40+ candidates hired for in one week.
- Resume: assets/KittyGao_Resume.pdf.
- Missing, must not be fabricated: her photo, her own-voice sentences, real sketches/sketchbook art, blog post links (Medium, Burnt Toast Thoughts @slightlyburnt), results for the picnic and puppy posters.

## Product Principles
1. Be memorable over complete: one strong impression beats a full inventory.
2. Show her real work and real hand; never pad with invented proof.
3. Skimmable in 30 seconds, rewarding for 5 minutes.
4. Personality through specifics (her cat-o, her languages, her side quests), not generic decoration.
