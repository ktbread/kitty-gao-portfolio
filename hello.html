October 2026

I built something, something that I’ve been wanting to build for a very long time.

When I started university, I felt like all of my creativity disappeared. Or maybe I shouldn’t say creativity, but the motivation to turn ideas into projects.

As a kid, and even up until the end of second year, I thought I belonged somewhere that let me explore and create with my own hands. I called myself a half “art kid”, and only half because art was just a hobby of mine, compared to those who built their career path around it. What changed my mind? I don’t have an exact answer to that. Maybe it’s just a part of growing up and conforming to reality, as “real” adults always said.

I’ve always struggled with trying to fit myself into a certain group. In high school, I was an “art kid” taking mostly STEM courses. In first and second year, I was a psychology student trying to do business. In third year, I was a business student also doing psychology. And now in my fourth year, who am I?

I had a really hard time starting on this. There were a lot of ideas and styles I wanted to try, and I eventually stressed myself out with all of the possibilities. It took me several weeks to finalize the logo, and by the end of it, I was already exhausted. So I quit, temporarily.

My creativity comes from observations, and observations take time. I had forgotten how long it’s supposed to take to create something I’m completely satisfied with. Weeks, months, maybe even years. I’d open my laptop and stare at the landing page every night, thinking to myself, “how do I make this better? something looks off.”

Eventually, I accepted that I may never be satisfied with what I made, but that’s ok. I can and will keep updating the site, with new art, new projects, or just the UI in general.

So, here’s my website. This is my attempt to reintroduce myself, to others and to myself. I can be both a psychology and a business student, and enjoy art and other hobbies on the side. Nothing that I do should define who I am, or lock me into a certain character.

Hi, I’m Kitty. Not Hello Kitty, just Kitty, a psychology and business student who still draws on the side. Nice to meet you!
